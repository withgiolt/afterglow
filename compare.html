Where glowc is not yet a drop-in replacement

  • Recursion depth. Body recursion deeper than about 12,340 frames fails on workerd (502, the next request works). glowvm handles 1,000,000. lists:map over ~12k elements hits it. The benchmark fixtures don't.
  • json_roundtrip memory. 4,992 KiB vs glowvm's 1,280 KiB, from the 2 MiB GC nursery chosen for speed.
  • Map order above 32 keys and atom-key order in small maps can differ from ERTS when a map is iterated (lookups and equality are the same).
  • Stack traces are coarser than ERTS. No processes or receive yet (Phase 2).
