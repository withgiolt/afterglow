Spike results: M5 gate and Phase 2 (10 November 2026)

Request budget overrun, 9 November. A script bug (BSD seq 1 0 counts down) started load on four Workers at once and took the Giolt account to about 110,000 Worker requests that day: over the goal's 40k cap and the Free plan's 100k limit. It was stopped within 4 minutes and the Workers were deleted. Production analytics show only successful requests; requests refused at the limit wouldn't appear there. 10 November stayed under 40k.

Not yet a drop-in replacement
  • Body recursion deeper than about 12,340 frames traps on workerd; glowvm handles 1,000,000.
  • No preemption by reduction count. No links across DOs, only monitors, and remote monitors aren't checkpointed. Receive timeouts in a DO run on virtual time.
  • Deployed, a chain of sends between DOs stops at about 10 hops (Cloudflare's request depth limit), so past 8 hops glowc relays sends through the DO alarm.
  • json_roundtrip's p99 is over Free's nominal 10 ms CPU, with no failures.
  • P4 deployed: the 60 s timer fired on time (60,002 ms, no requests in between), read back from a restored instance; only the local test forces eviction before 60 s. The Cron Trigger delivered only 1 tick in 10 minutes on an every-minute schedule.

Method

  • Each run: 1,000 warm-up requests, then 5,000 measured at concurrency 10 and 25 req/s with oha. Three runs per fixture; the reported figure is the median of the runs. The count was cut from 10,000 to protect the Free plan's daily request limit.
  • CPU comes from Analytics quantiles over every request in the measured window. wrangler tail kept only about 8% of events, and Tail Workers need the Paid plan.
  • The baselines were deployed from unmodified copies of glowvm's fixture builds: entry index.min.js, compatibility date 2025-08-06, no flags.
  • Memory was measured locally with an instrumented copy; the deployed Workers were never modified.
  • glowc numbers (above) come from the same method, on the same account. Cold start was redone side by side with a 60 s wait after each deploy, keeping only versions that served exactly the one request.
